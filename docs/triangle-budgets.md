# Triangle budgets

Triangle counts at LOD0 for every block, against the game's limits. Generated from the committed
`Source/Model/Models/<block>/export.json` files; refresh it after a re-export with
`python3 Source/Model/dc/budget_table.py`. `Tests/test_states_docs.py` fails when the table is out of date or a block
is over its limit.

- **NF:** the plain (undamaged) model.
- **FR LOD0:** the fractured model, including the construction cage (scaffold) and the still copies of moving parts
  (stand-ins). **FR limit** is the game's limit for a fractured model of that size.
- **DM LOD0:** the damaged model; its limit is the fractured limit, which the build enforces.
- **FR LODs:** how many LODs the fractured model has.

The four blocks that do not break (the Long Drop Chute and the three Hanger Rods) have no cage and use a debris model
as their damaged model.
